About Nest a Nook

Reading, learning and gifting, done kindly.

At Nest a Nook, we believe reading, learning, and gifting should be meaningful, cost-effective, and kind to our planet. Based in Qatar, Nest a Nook is a sustainable e-commerce destination offering a carefully chosen mix of pre-loved and new books, educational puzzles, interactive games, creative craft kits, and custom-tailored book hampers for readers and learners of all ages.

Our mission is centered on "Gifts with a Purpose": providing budget-friendly, sustainable options that encourage smart financial thinking in kids, inspire lifelong reading, and give pre-loved books and toys a second life. We place a strong emphasis on reducing screen time for children by offering hands-on, mind-expanding activities like skill-building puzzles and Plaster of Paris painting sets.

From fully customizable gift boxes equipped with bookmarks, study accessories, and cozy touches to engaging learning tools, every package from Nest a Nook is designed to foster creativity, screen-free enjoyment, and mindful living.
